# Sankhara

### Evidence-Based Manufacturing Intelligence & Decision Support

**Sankhara** is an early-stage manufacturing intelligence and decision-support software initiative focused on helping small and medium manufacturing enterprises make more reliable engineering and operational decisions.

Our objective is to make rigorous manufacturing analytics practical, understandable, and accessible.

### Areas of Focus

- Manufacturing data quality and statistical analysis
- Statistical Process Control (SPC)
- Process capability and process variation
- Measurement Systems Analysis (MSA)
- Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
- Regression and Design of Experiments (DOE)
- Manufacturing quality and process improvement
- Evidence-based operational decision support
- Responsible AI applications in manufacturing

### Our Approach
- We believe trustworthy manufacturing intelligence should:
- Distinguish observations, hypotheses, and verified conclusions.
- Communicate uncertainty and limitations clearly.
- Support engineering judgment rather than replace it.
- Translate analytical evidence into practical manufacturing understanding.
- Help manufacturers investigate problems and improve decisions responsibly.

### Development Status

Sankhara is currently under active development and technical validation.

The project is not yet a commercially released or independently validated software platform.
